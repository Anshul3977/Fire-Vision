Fire-Vision

Drop in a photo or a short clip and a YOLOv8n detector marks fire and smoke. It was fine-tuned on the D-Fire wildfire dataset and runs on ONNX Runtime on a CPU serverless function, with no GPU and no PyTorch at serve time.

Clips are sampled in your browser (12 frames), and each frame goes to /api/detect. An alert would fire after 3 consecutive positive frames, which is the same rule as the server-side /api/detect/video endpoint. The colour-rule pixel ratios come from the original OpenCV pipeline, ported to NumPy, and are a cheap second signal. In the full app an alert sends a Twilio SMS and call; here it only reports "would alert".
