# Bundled sample clip

`sample_fire.mp4` is a short H.264 re-encode of a Wikimedia Commons video so the demo can play it in a browser and in OpenCV.

- Title: Mountain Forest Fire in Pauri Garhwal District
- Author: RachnaChaudhary1
- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mountain_Forest_Fire_in_Pauri_Garhwal_District.webm
- License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
- License URL: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0
- Original duration: 6.7 seconds, 848×478. This copy is 640×360, no audio.

Changes: scaled to 640×360, audio removed, encoded as H.264. Under CC BY-SA 4.0 this file stays under that license.
